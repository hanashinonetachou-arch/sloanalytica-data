# Revue LED lower-bound proof audit — v8.3

## Question

Can the published CZ occurrence count be used as a guaranteed lower bound for the setting-difference LED trial universe?

## Fresh source semantics

Multiple public analysis sources agree on the following:
- the setting-difference LED color distribution is for fake-CZ-premonition end / CZ end;
- AT-end LED is a distinct context with different semantics;
- one source explicitly labels the distribution as "(フェイク前兆終了・CZ失敗時)";
- another source labels the table "CZ終了時" and adds "設定変更時・AT終了後を除く".

These sources establish that an ordinary failed-CZ end produces the relevant LED observation. They do not establish that every event counted in the published aggregate CZ occurrence rate necessarily ends as a failed CZ. A CZ success can transition to AT, and the available setting-difference LED table is not explicitly stated to apply to successful-CZ endings.

## Decision

The previous implication

    every published CZ occurrence => one eligible setting-difference LED trial

is NOT proven.

Therefore:
- the earlier CZ-rate-derived lower bounds (26.3257 ... 38.9972 at 7000G) are withdrawn as benchmark-authoritative;
- the derived GuaranteedMinimumIG / GuaranteedMinimumSelectionScore (~92.55 under the v8.3 deterministic rule) is withdrawn;
- LED remains a valid exhaustive LIVE_CONDITIONAL multinomial observation because its exact live denominator is the sum of the five eligible observed colors;
- benchmark exposure for LED returns to UNRESOLVED unless a public rate/count for eligible failed-CZ endings, or another proven contained event class, is independently found.

## Manifest consequence

No new v8.3 rule is required. EXP-014B already requires the source-supported implication/containment relation and exclusions. The replay demonstrates that the fail-closed proof obligation works: deterministic lower-bound scoring cannot rescue an unproven lower-bound premise.

This correction is machine Research/Exposure, not a machine-specific exception.
