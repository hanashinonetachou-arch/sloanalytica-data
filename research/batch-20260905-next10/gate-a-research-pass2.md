## Source classes

- `PUBLISHED`: exact machine-specific values/context published by established machine-analysis sources and cross-checked where practical.
- `EMPIRICAL`: field/practical values or incomplete samples; never promoted to a six-setting table.
- `INDICATION`: setting hint / lower-bound / exact-setting evidence without a complete setting-conditioned appearance distribution.
- `STRUCTURAL`: machine-process fact used to define denominator, context or exclusions.
- `UNRESOLVED`: machine-specific public evidence remains insufficient.
