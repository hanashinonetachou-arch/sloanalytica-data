Status: PASS

This document closes Gate B. Selection is based on publication quality, observability, denominator integrity, information value, counting burden and dependency risk. EvidenceEngine items are separated from statistical likelihood features.

## Decision vocabulary

- ADOPT: primary statistical likelihood feature for Observation/implementation.
- SUPPORT: retained research candidate, but not part of the primary likelihood core; may be surfaced as reference/diagnostic or promoted only after stronger observability/dependency evidence.
- EVIDENCE: lower-bound / exact-setting / setting-denial / indication observation handled outside ordinary likelihood multiplication.
- REJECT: not used as a setting feature in the current version.
