# Manifest v8.5 Research responsibility and completion

Authority: explicit user instruction of 2026-10-10; preserves existing Manifest and Candidate Contract. This is not a new statistical model or a relaxation of provenance checks.

Research confirms sources, values, observed events, acquisition conditions and dependencies. Implementation handled by an existing Contract must not become an additional source acquisition requirement. Known overlap proceeds to Candidate Contract: score-based representative selection or explicitly ranked preferred/fallback, never independent simultaneous scoring. Existing approved conditional models remain unchanged.

Use the user counting policy for initial hits and excluded games. Published denominator equivalence stays NOT_ASSERTED; exact publisher equivalence or restoration of every internal path is not an adoption gate and must not be claimed. Do not invent zone boundaries, latent values, conditional rates or joint distributions.

Unobservable conditional trials, unavailable values and conflicting documents close with retained values, sources and plain-language exclusion reasons. Public search closure is a bounded survey, not a proof of universal absence. Reopen only for a concrete new source.

Every new stop records its authority/basis, responsible stage and release condition. AI may not tighten Manifest/Contract completion unilaterally. Implementation, missing information and human-only decisions are separate queues. After a resolution, synchronize active findings, blocks, ledger, domains and queue; retain old notes only as explicitly historical snapshots.

Report source review, value confirmation, survey closure/exclusion, user-definition resolution and formal stage acceptance separately. Coverage and passing tests do not imply stage approval or Runtime publication. Evidence source review is AI work, not per-stage user approval.

Tests protect confirmed rules, source integrity, observation accuracy and duplicate prevention. Historical unresolved fixtures do not define current production completion. Current Batch regressions must exercise the real corrected Research through builders and validators. Existing stopping conditions are corrected with recorded reasons rather than bypassing preflight.

Proceed through Research → Evaluation → Eligibility → Candidate Contract → Observation/Evidence → Canonical UI → Runtime → Distribution → APK → device QA. Normal commits use [skip ci]; Actions run at Integration Boundary. Batch008 distribution stays protected. A completed audit, commit or test suite is not a stopping point.

## Candidate and domain review semantics

Record discovered setting-inference candidates before Evaluation or Eligibility.
Do not reduce Research to one representative candidate: dependent, weak and
unusable candidates retain their source claims and existing downstream routes.
Each source claim attached to a ledger row must concern that candidate, rather
than merely appearing somewhere in the same overview article.

`NOT_APPLICABLE` means the domain does not apply to this machine's mechanics.
Missing published setting values or a lack of adoptable candidates are survey
outcomes, not structural inapplicability. Record the observed candidates and
their existing exclusion/closure routes. `CHECKED` notes describe the observed
mechanic or review result; shared assertions that a review happened are not
review evidence. No candidate-count quota, universal absence proof or new
mandatory publisher-denominator equivalence is introduced.

Mechanical validation rejects explicit placeholders and inconsistent recorded
routes. Source review remains responsible for checking the overview's candidate
headings against the ledger; validator success alone cannot establish semantic
completeness. The source-conflict and bounded public-search rules remain in force.

## Canonical UI observation-opportunity contract (2026-10-11)

For multiple setting-inference findings observed at the same occasion, Research may declare a strictly presentation-only `observationContext` on each finding:
`{ "groupingApproved": true, "opportunityId": "stable_machine_local_key", "conditionId": "distinct_condition_key", "groupTitle": "shared Japanese heading", "conditionLabel": "short Japanese tab label" }`.

The generator groups only two or more approved sections sharing the exact opportunityId with distinct conditionId and conditionLabel and a common groupTitle. Do not infer these fields from similar titles or matching denominators. If the observation opportunity is uncertain, omit the metadata and retain separate sections until reviewed. Existing curated `uiPresentation.tabGroups` remains authoritative and is not replaced by generated groups.

The opportunity context describes *where an event was observed*, not the denominator or probability model. Do not merge different trial universes, distributions, dependency groups, counts, evidence semantics, inputIds or exact-setting constraints. A single observation must not be copied into multiple tab groups; already curated sections are excluded from generated grouping.

Before enabling grouping, Research must verify whether events are exclusive or overlapping, the observation period, numerical denominator, trigger and presentation labels. A context-only change must not re-materialize older unreviewed Research over audited Runtime. Golden regressions cover existing curated groups, numeric inputs and Evidence, and old saved inputs retain their IDs.


## Source-owned concise input labels (2026-10-13)

Research may declare optional `inputPresentation: { trialLabel, successLabel }`
for a numeric finding. Both labels must be nonempty Japanese user-facing text
of at most 30 characters. Observation/Evidence preserves this presentation
metadata and Canonical UI uses it before its existing label inference fallback.
The field never changes a denominator, model, input ID, dependency, score,
setting constraint or quick-add policy. Detailed scope and exclusions remain
in `denominatorSemantics` and the generated description. Game-count +50 remains
controlled by the established trial-universe rule; event counts do not gain +50
from a label. Existing findings without this metadata use the unchanged fallback.


## 全項目の出現率が未確認の参考入力（2026-10-16 ユーザー確定）

今後の全機種・全Batchで、セクション内の全項目が設定別出現率未公表・未確認の参考入力である場合、セクション内に次の説明を必ず明記する。

> このセクションの全項目は設定別出現率が公表・確認されていないため、記録のみです。設定推測の計算には使用しません。

対象は全カテゴリが `PROBABILITY_UNKNOWN` または `DISPLAY_ONLY` のセクション。ボーナス終了ミニキャラ等の今回5セクションだけに限定しない。未知の出現率に重みを付けず、入力は記録として保持する。

設定確定・設定否定（`EXACT_CONSTRAINT`）を含むセクションは、設定候補の絞り込みへの反映を維持し、全項目が記録のみとは表示しない。既知の出現率で評価する項目と未知の項目が混在する場合も、セクション全体を記録のみと説明しない。

共通実装は `src/canonical-ui-builder.ts`、継続適用の回帰テストは `test/canonical-ui.test.ts`。将来の機種生成・共通処理修正・チャット引き継ぎではこの規約を継承する。採否閾値や評価経路は変更しない。

## Source-neutral product copy (user decision 2026-10-17)

Never expose referenced website/publisher names or source URLs in user-facing reasons, explanations, labels, or guidance. Explain the actual adoption/rejection reason, observation limits, conflicting settings/values, and their inference effect in plain language. If contrasting sources is necessary, use anonymous public-document references. Keep original publisher names, URLs, exact claims, and conflict history in research/audit artifacts for reproducibility. Do not change probabilities, eligibility, or exact constraints to remove attribution. Apply this to existing and future machines; test actual rendered text as well as packaged summary adapters.
